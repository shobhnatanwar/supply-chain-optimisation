# **Supply Chain Optimization**


## **Project Overview**

Supply chains generate enormous operational data that often goes underanalyzed.
This project ingests raw order-level records, applies structured transformations, and surfaces actionable intelligence across four analytical domains
that is, cost optimization, delivery performance, carrier efficiency, and supplier risk through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to identify operational bottlenecks, monitor supplier and carrier performance, reduce logistics costs, improve on-time delivery rates, and support data-driven decision making.

The project simulates a real-world business intelligence workflow, covering the complete analytics lifecycle from data preparation to executive reporting.


## **Business Problem**

Supply chain operations often face challenges such as:

1. Rising logistics costs
2. Delivery delays and SLA breaches
3. Supplier reliability issues
4. Procurement risks
5. Regional performance inconsistencies
6. Limited visibility into operational inefficiencies


## **Objectives**

1. Analyze logistics costs and profitability drivers
2. Evaluate delivery performance across regions and carriers
3. Identify high-risk suppliers impacting operations
4. Monitor SLA compliance and delay trends
5. Measure procurement efficiency using supplier scorecards
6. Detect cost leakages and operational inefficiencies
7. Support strategic and operational decision-making

## **Dashboard Architecture**

The dashboard is divided into four analytical modules:

### 1️. **Executive Operations Overview**

Provides a high-level view of overall supply chain performance.

Key Metrics
1. Total Revenue
2. Total Orders
3. Total Shipping Cost
4. On-Time Delivery %
5. Average Delivery Days
6. Average Delay Days
7. Analysis Included
8. Revenue Trend Analysis
9. Carrier Performance Ranking
10. Regional Performance Comparison
11. Delivery Trend Monitoring

Enables executives to quickly assess overall operational health and identify areas requiring attention.

### 2️. **Delivery Performance & SLA Analysis**

Focuses on delivery reliability and service performance.

Key Metrics
1. On-Time Orders
2. Late Orders
3. Average Delay Days
4. SLA Breach Percentage
5. Analysis Included
6. Delay Trend Analysis
7. Regional Delay Comparison
8. Carrier Delay Ranking
9. SLA Achievement Tracking
10. Monthly Delay Heatmaps

Helps identify delay drivers, monitor SLA compliance, and improve customer satisfaction.

### 3️. **Supplier Performance & Procurement Risk**

Evaluates supplier reliability and procurement effectiveness.

Key Metrics
1. Total Suppliers
2. Average Lead Time
3. Best Performing Supplier
4. Worst Performing Supplier
5. High-Risk Suppliers
6. Average Defect Rate
7. Analysis Included
8. Supplier Lead Time Ranking
9. Supplier Defect Analysis
10. Procurement Risk Matrix
11. Supplier Contribution to Delays
12. Supplier Performance Scorecard

Supports supplier evaluation, procurement planning, and risk mitigation strategies.

### 4️. **Cost Optimization & Profitability Analysis**

Analyzes operational costs and profitability drivers.

Key Metrics
1. Total Logistics Cost
2. Average Cost per KM
3. Cost Efficiency Score
4. Delay Cost
5. Analysis Included
6. Logistics Cost Trends
7. Delay Cost Analysis
8. Carrier Cost Efficiency Ranking
9. Regional Cost Comparison
10. Cost Driver Analysis

Highlights opportunities to reduce operational expenses and improve profitability.


## **Key Insights**

### 1. **Logistics Costs**

- Significant cost variation exists across regions.
- Delay-related costs contribute heavily to total logistics expenditure.

### 2. **Delivery Performance**
   
- Certain carriers consistently outperform others in delivery reliability.
- SLA achievement varies significantly across months.

### 3. **Supplier Performance**

- A small number of suppliers contribute disproportionately to operational delays.
- High lead times are strongly associated with increased delivery risk.

### 4. **Regional Operations**

- Regional performance disparities reveal opportunities for process optimization.
- Specific regions exhibit recurring delay patterns.

## **Potential Business Impact**

Implementation of insights generated through this dashboard can help organizations:

1. Improve On-Time Delivery Performance
2. Reduce Logistics Costs
3. Optimize Carrier Selection
4. Improve Supplier Management
5. Minimize Procurement Risks
6. Increase Operational Efficiency
7. Support Strategic Decision-Making
